# Hoop Roulette — brand and rights review

Working guidance checked 2026-10-26. This is a preliminary research note, not a trademark clearance opinion or a filing.

## What each right covers

A product name is generally a trademark question. Copyright does not protect names or short slogans in the US; sufficiently original artwork may qualify. Original human-authored software and artwork can be protected, but game ideas, systems and methods are excluded. AI-generated material requires a separate assessment of human authorship; do not assume every generated asset is copyrightable.

Official sources: [Copyright Office FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html), [17 USC 102](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t17/s102/), [Copyright Office AI initiative](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/).

## Name clearance comes before registration

No formal trademark database clearance has been completed. An empty exact-name web search is insufficient: similar spelling, pronunciation, meaning and related goods/services can matter. Search Hoop Roulette, HoopRoulette, Hoops Roulette and relevant Korean variants. Search existing apps, domains and unregistered uses as well as trademark databases. Domain or GitHub availability does not establish trademark rights.

Start with [KIPRIS](https://www.kipris.or.kr/) for Korea, [USPTO search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search) for the US, and [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database). [USPTO guidance on similar marks](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-search-similar-trademarks) explains why exact matching is inadequate.

For a Korea-first launch, ask a Korean trademark professional to assess the word mark and intended markets before filing. Downloadable game software commonly falls in Class 9; online game entertainment may involve Class 41. Final classes and goods/services wording depend on the actual offering. Registration is territorial; there is no universal worldwide registration. A word-mark application and a logo application protect different presentations and should be scoped deliberately.

Sources: [Korean registration system](https://www.kipo.go.kr/en/HtmlApp?c=30103&catmenu=ek04_02_01), [WIPO trademarks](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/trademarks), [WIPO goods and services](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/identify-trademark-goods-and-services).

Do not use ® before applicable registration. Under US guidance, TM can identify a claimed mark without registration; it is not proof of clearance. [USPTO trademark basics](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/what-trademark).

## Third-party content is a separate review

NBA.com's terms restrict statistics reuse, including fantasy-game and commercial uses. [NBA terms](https://www.nba.com/termsofuse).

Renaming the app does not obtain rights to NBA/team marks, player imagery or likenesses, or a source database. Factual statistics and the conditions under which a dataset was obtained are separate questions. Audit each source's license and terms, redistribution permissions, and any attribution obligations before wider distribution or monetization. A free build or an independent-fan disclaimer does not itself grant permission. Keep original generic artwork and avoid implying official affiliation.

The game mechanics can be described in generic terms. Do not copy another game's rulebook wording, artwork or brand presentation.
