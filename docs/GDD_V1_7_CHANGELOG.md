# GDD v1.7 — Rotation Player Eligibility

Implemented in v0.8.

Normal entries require at least 30 regular-season games AND 15.0 MPG for the selected team. Applies to all three current seasons and future normal collection. Supersedes the proposed special older-era cutoff for now. GOAT pools retain their existing separate criteria.

Current counts: 2023–24: 300; 2024–25: 317; 2025–26: 326. Total: 943 (previously 1,346). All 90 team-seasons remain represented; roster sizes 7–14. The original dataset is preserved in data/legacy/15gp_10mpg. Filtering uses existing published one-decimal MPG.

The 120 → +35 bonus, unique-player draft exclusions and all other scoring rules remain unchanged. Historical balance reports have not been recalculated. Playtest Batch 8 separates results for the tighter pool.
